# RareImagery — Pricing & X Integration

> RareImagery is a marketplace built *for* X creators — not a replacement for X.
> We follow all X platform rules and policies. Nothing in this platform competes with or circumvents X's native features.

---

## Platform Fees (Collected by RareImagery)

| Fee | Amount | When | What It Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Store Setup | $5 (one-time) | At launch | Creator storefront creation on `[creator].rareimagery.net` |
| Store Maintenance | $2/mo | Monthly | Hosting, subdomain routing, Drupal/Next.js infrastructure |

These are the only fees RareImagery charges. There are no hidden fees, revenue cuts on sales, or additional platform charges beyond the $1 POD fulfillment cost per order.

---

## X Subscriptions (Managed Entirely by X)

RareImagery does **not** collect, manage, or control X Subscriptions in any way.

| Detail | Notes |
|---|---|
| Price | $2/mo |
| Set by | Creator, on X |
| Collected by | X (via Stripe) |
| Paid out by | X, directly to creator |
| Managed via | X Creator Studio / Spaces |
| RareImagery's role | None — we link to X, that's it |

X Subscriptions are a native X product. Creators enable them in X Creator Studio and run them through X Spaces. RareImagery surfaces a link to the creator's X subscription page from their storefront — nothing more.

---

## What RareImagery Is

A storefront layer on top of X. Creators bring their X audience to a branded store at `[creator].rareimagery.net` where fans can browse and buy physical products (print-on-demand). The platform:

- Uses X OAuth for login (no separate account needed)
- Imports X profile data (avatar, banner, bio) via xAI Grok to populate the store
- Gives creators a MySpace-era themed storefront that reflects their X identity
- Handles POD fulfillment at $1/order cost passthrough

---

## What RareImagery Is Not

- Not a replacement for X Spaces or X Subscriptions
- Not an alternative DM inbox or notification system
- Not a competing creator monetization platform
- Not a tool to circumvent any X feature, policy, or native product

---

## Compliance

RareImagery is built and launched in full compliance with X's platform rules and developer policies. Any feature that could conflict with X's terms of service will not be shipped. When in doubt, we defer to X's guidelines.

---

## Summary

```
Creator pays RareImagery:  $5 one-time + $2/mo
Creator earns from X:      $2/mo per subscriber (X handles all of this)
RareImagery earns from POD: $1/order fulfilled
```

Simple, transparent, and fully compliant.
